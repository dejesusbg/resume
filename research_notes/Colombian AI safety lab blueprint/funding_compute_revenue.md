# Funding, Compute and Early Revenue for a Zero-Budget AI Safety Lab Founder (Colombia / LatAm focus) — status as of 31 Oct 2026

Method note: Research was done on 31 Oct 2026 by web search and fetch. Several official funder pages (coefficientgiving.org, the EA Forum and LessWrong) blocked fetches with 403, 429 or 404 errors. Where that happened I relied on search snippets, the Manifund "AI Safety Funder Bulletin" (dated 15 Oct 2026, the best single aggregator found) and grant-aggregator listings such as grantedai.com. These are flagged inline. Treat aggregator-only figures as "verify before relying on them".

## 1. AI safety philanthropic funders: 2025–2026 status, sizes, and eligibility outside the US/UK

### Takeaway
As of late 2026 the money is larger and more founder-friendly than ever. The most accessible entry points for a Colombia-based founder with no money are:
- Coefficient Giving's Project Tailwind: pre-seed $200k–$2M, no legal entity needed, non-US teams eligible.
- BlueDot Impact's grant suite: Rapid grants up to $20k in about 5 days, Career Transition up to $200k, Seed $20k–$200k.
- EA Funds' Transformative AI Fund (TAIF, which replaced LTFF in Aug 2026): $10k–$150k.
- The new GTR AI Safety Fund: €50M, global.
- Lightcone Commons: accepts individuals and unincorporated groups, quarterly rounds.

SFF and the UK AISI Alignment Project are larger but need an organisation or fiscal sponsor, and AISI's grant programmes are currently closed.

### Cited Findings

**Coefficient Giving (formerly Open Philanthropy)**
- The rename is confirmed: Open Philanthropy became Coefficient Giving in November 2025, a change that signals a move toward multi-donor funds — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coefficient_Giving); [GrantedAI blog, 23 Sep 2026](https://grantedai.com/blog/coefficient-giving-project-tailwind-200-million-launchpad-funds-50-million-october-2-2026-eoi-deadline-general-manager-model-pre-seed-no-legal-entity-strategy).
- Spending: AI safety and security spending more than doubled in 2025 to $351M, and Coefficient says it is "on track to commit over $1 billion this year" (2026) — [Coefficient Giving (search snippet of official site)](https://coefficientgiving.org/research/were-urgently-scaling-our-work-on-ai-and-biosecurity/). The Manifund bulletin says Coefficient directed about $409M through its two AIS-relevant public funds in 2025, with grant sizes from $12k to $160M — [Manifund AI Safety Funder Bulletin, 15 Oct 2026](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).
- **Project Tailwind** was launched 9 Sep 2026 under the Navigating Transformative AI fund with about $200M. It is a call for founders to launch new AI safety organisations from their own idea or from Coefficient's list:
  - Ideas on the list: rogue incident detection, agreement/verification technology, a misalignment red team, a frontier capabilities observatory, an independent AI auditing body, and talent and convening organisations.
  - Tiers: pre-seed $200k–$2M, seed $2M–$20M, scale $20M–$200M.
  - Eligibility: pre-seed "needs no legal entity", and non-US teams and public-benefit for-profits (PBCs/PBLLCs) are eligible. For-profits are funded via program-related investments or expenditure responsibility.
  - Deadline: rolling. A skilled operator without an ML background can qualify.
  - Sources: [GrantedAI blog](https://grantedai.com/blog/coefficient-giving-project-tailwind-200-million-launchpad-funds-50-million-october-2-2026-eoi-deadline-general-manager-model-pre-seed-no-legal-entity-strategy); [AISafety.com funding newsletter, 16 Sep 2026](https://aisafetyfunding.substack.com/p/issue-20-2026); [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).
- Tailwind FAQ (official, via search snippet): seed is $2M–$20M "to let a founding team launch and scale an organization over 18 to 36 months". Coefficient automatically filters out submissions not related to catastrophic AI risk or that "appear AI-generated", does not expect to respond to every submission, and runs a talent-scout bounty for founder referrals — [Coefficient Giving Tailwind FAQs](https://coefficientgiving.org/tailwind/faqs/); [Tailwind main page](https://coefficientgiving.org/tailwind/).
- **Technical AI Safety RFP:** roughly $40M across 21 research areas, including interpretability, scalable oversight and reward hacking. Its status is **conflicting**. One aggregator lists it as closed with a deadline of 21 Aug 2026 [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/coefficient-giving-rfp-technical-ai-safety-research-navigating-transformative-ai-coefficient-2026); others list it as rolling [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/technical-ai-safety-coefficient-giving-coefficient-giving-36d90d5c). The Manifund bulletin lists only "two open RFPs, capacity-building and programs/events", with decisions "within 3 months" [Manifund](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).
- The AI Governance RFP closed 25 Jan 2026 per one aggregator [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/coefficient-giving-ai-governance-research-rfp-coefficient-giving-f6a7b8c9). This is contradicted by another listing describing it as open indefinitely [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/ai-governance-and-policy-rfp-open-philanthropy-now-coefficient-giving-2a0c0719).
- Coefficient's career transition funding **moved to BlueDot Impact on 4 Sep 2026** — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).

**BlueDot Impact (now a major grantmaker)**
- About $7.97M granted across 1,163 grants as of 15 Oct 2026, and $71M raised in total per its CEO — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).
- Grant lines:
  - Career Transition: up to $200k, decision in about 20 days.
  - Rapid: up to $20k, decision in about 5 days.
  - Seed: $20k–$200k, decision in about 4 weeks.
  - Incubator Week: applications due 11 Nov 2026 for the 16–20 Nov 2026 cohort.
  - Source: [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).
- An earlier 2026 listing gave the Rapid grant cap as $10k, with decisions in 5 working days. It said "stipends and living expenses aren't our usual scope" but applicants can make the case — [Opportunities for Youth, Apr 2026](https://opportunitiesforyouth.org/2026/04/22/bluedot-impact-rapid-grants-2026-get-up-to-10000-quickly-to-power-your-ai-safety-project/?amp=1). This conflicts with the bulletin's $20k cap, which is probably a later increase.
- Career Transition grants give "6-12 months of financial runway" for upskilling and exploring, with rolling applications — [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/career-transition-grants-bluedot-impact-eb4e3d88).

**Transformative AI Fund (TAIF), the successor to the Long-Term Future Fund (LTFF)**
- LTFF closed in August 2026 with about $3.7M left: about $2.8M went to existing applicants, mostly via the first Lightcone Commons round, and about $0.9M seeded TAIF.
- TAIF is run by EA Funds, headed by Lowe Lundin, and accepts rolling applications.
- Grants are typically $10k–$150k and rarely above $300k, with a 6–8 week response time. No TAIF grants were public as of 1 Oct 2026.
- Sources: [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin); [AISafety.com newsletter](https://aisafetyfunding.substack.com/p/issue-20-2026).

**Survival and Flourishing Fund (SFF, funded by Jaan Tallinn and Dustin Moskovitz)**
- 2026 Main Round: closed 22 Apr 2026. Recommendations published 30 Sep 2026: $63.13M across 115 grants, above the $14–28M estimate. Speculation Grants are open. SFF also launched a $5–20M "ISPA Inspection Prize" with no deadline — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin). The 2025 round was about $34.33M per a secondary source — [AISafety.com newsletter search snippet](https://aisafetyfunding.substack.com/p/ai-safety-funding-2026-15).
- Eligibility: individuals without a fiscal sponsor are **not** eligible — [EffectiveAltruism.org opportunity listing](https://www.effectivealtruism.org/opportunities/recmtTB4opD50U2UF). Aggregators say incorporated for-profits or nonprofits "globally" may apply, excluding adversarial nations. For-profit funding is confirmed only for the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and other for-profits may face extra requirements — [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/survival-and-flourishing-fund-ai-safety-grants-sff-b2c3d4e5).
- A Speculation Grant guarantees S-Process consideration, and an aggregator says over 95% of evaluated applications received one in past rounds — [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/speculation-grants-survival-and-flourishing-fund-sff-259436a4). One applicant reports that SFF required for-profits to be incorporated with a company bank account — [Effective Ink blog, Apr 2026](https://effectiveink.blogspot.com/2026/04/what-i-learned-about-ea-funding-by.html).

**Lightcone Commons (new, July 2026)**
- Launched 23 Jul 2026. It accepts applications from nonprofits, for-profits, unincorporated groups and private individuals, and uses the S-Process — [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/lightcone-commons-quarterly-grant-rounds-ai-alignment-lightcone-9d4f2b18); [LessWrong announcement](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/fYostss6JqkSfxc5C/introducing-lightcone-commons).
- First-round applications closed 23 Aug 2026, and recommendations were expected around 23 Oct.
  - Round size: Nathan Young said (12 Oct) it "looks set to advise on $50m this round". An earlier estimate was $15–25M — [TechTimes, 25 Jul 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321586/20260725/lightcone-commons-launches-new-algorithm-aims-fix-ai-safety-philanthropy.htm).
  - Fee: 5% of grants.
  - Next rounds: a second round "before Christmas" 2026, with possible monthly rounds from Q1 2027.
  - Imported applications: it pulls in applications from Manifund, LTFF and grantmaking.ai.
  - Source: [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).

**Manifund (regranting platform)**
- $6.91M donated in 2026 through 14 Oct. Grants range from $0 to $500k, and projects are posted publicly.
- Its Falcon Fund is rolling, decides in about 1 week and grants $25k–$150k, but it covers animal welfare plus transformative AI rather than AIS as such — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).
- About $6M of AI-safety grantmaking in 2025 and $8.8M projected for 2026 — [Manifund via search snippet](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).

**GTR AI Safety Fund (new)**
- Launched 14 Sep 2026 with a €50M commitment over 3–7 years from Jan Beckers.
- Rolling applications: a 1–3 page proposal plus CV, with initial feedback targeted within 7 days.
- "No geographic focus"; funds individuals and early-stage orgs. Grant sizes are not published — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).

**Macroscopic and Astralis Foundation**
- Macroscopic plans "up to $100m" in 2026.
- Their joint Middle Powers and Transformative AI RFP is about $10M, with grants of $100k–$2M. It was open until 23 Oct 2026 (now closed or rolling-review only).
- Eligible applicants include individuals, informal teams, universities, fiscally sponsored orgs, and nonprofit projects inside for-profits. Astralis takes no unsolicited requests — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).

**Halcyon Futures (nonprofit grantmaker, incubator and VC hybrid)**
- Per the 15 Oct 2026 bulletin, its ceilings have been raised: career transition up to $500k, nonprofit seed up to $3M, VC up to $5M. Contact is by email only — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).
- An earlier aggregator lists career transition up to $500k and nonprofit seed up to $1M. It also claims Halcyon has incubated 20 organisations, which is unverified — [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/halcyon-futures-ai-safety-incubation-seed-funding-program-halcyon-c1d2e3f4).

**UK AI Security Institute (AISI)**
- The **Alignment Project** was launched in 2025 as "a new fund... backed by over £15 million", with up to £1M per project plus compute access, VC investment and expert support — [AISI on X](https://x.com/AISecurityInst/status/1950470166841872812).
  - Its first round reportedly awarded £27M to over 60 projects, with OpenAI contributing to the expansion — [aisecurityandsafety.org](https://aisecurityandsafety.org/en/grants/uk-aisi-alignment-project/).
  - It is now described as a pooled fund of over £27M backed by AISI, Schmidt Sciences, Halcyon Futures, Anthropic and OpenAI — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).
- Alignment Project eligibility: academic institutions and nonprofit research organisations, with awards of £50k–£1M. Its FAQ states there is **no restriction on applicants' nationality**, subject to an Eligibility Statement and due diligence — [Alignment Project Clarification Questions PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68752402cd25ead7e36d7373/689f625d71cc8e3bf7171978_The%20Alignment%20Project%20-%20Clarification%20Questions%20V2.0.pdf); [CIFAR, 5 Aug 2025](https://cifar.ca/cifarnews/2025/08/05/calls-open-for-global-ai-alignment-research-initiative/).
- **All three AISI programmes are closed**:
  - Alignment Project: "Applications are now closed."
  - Challenge Fund: up to £200k per project, open to UK *and international* academic and nonprofit organisations, launched 5 Mar 2025; now closed.
  - Systemic Safety Grants: the first 20 projects are underway; closed.
  - The page says AISI is not currently accepting applications — [AISI Grants page](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/grants).

**Schmidt Sciences**
- $10M to AI safety in 2025 across 27 projects, and an estimated $20M for its 2026 RFPs.
- All three 2026 AI safety RFPs have closed: trustworthy AI (17 May), interpretability (26 May) and multi-agent safety (9 Aug). No awards had been announced as of 15 Oct 2026.

- **Foresight Institute AI Safety Nodes RFP:** deadline 31 Oct 2026 (today); typically $30k–$100k; prefers in-person members of its SF or Berlin hubs — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin).

It is limited to **US or UK applicants only** and carries commercial terms, including a licence for Mercor and a 12-month competitor restriction — [Manifund bulletin](https://manifund.org/ais-funder-bulletin); [AISafety.com newsletter](https://aisafetyfunding.substack.com/p/issue-20-2026).

- **Anthropic-funded external programs:** the Anthropic Fellows Program (see section 2), the External Researcher Access Program and the AI for Science credits (section 3), and the Model Safety Bug Bounty (section 6). - **Funding concentration:** one overview describes the field as "highly concentrated among a few donors" — [LongtermWiki](https://www.longtermwiki.com/wiki/E641).

### Inferences
- **Best "first dollar" sources for a Colombian founder**, ranked by speed and accessibility:
  1. BlueDot Rapid (≤$20k, about 5 days).
  2. BlueDot Career Transition (≤$200k, about 20 days; it now holds Coefficient's former career-transition budget).
  3. TAIF ($10k–$150k, 6–8 weeks).
  4. GTR AI Safety Fund (global, 7-day initial feedback).
  5. A public Manifund project page, which also gets imported into Lightcone Commons.

  None of these sources states a US/UK residency requirement. GTR explicitly says "no geographic focus".
- **Best "seed team" sources:** Coefficient Tailwind pre-seed ($200k–$2M, no entity needed, non-US explicitly eligible); BlueDot Seed ($20k–$200k); Halcyon (nonprofit seed up to $3M); Lightcone Commons quarterly rounds; and future Macroscopic/Astralis "Middle Powers" calls. The "Middle Powers" framing plausibly fits a Latin American lab, though this is an inference from the RFP name only.
- **Organisation-stage sources:** SFF (needs an incorporated org or a fiscal sponsor), re-opened AISI rounds (need nonprofit or academic status, nationality unrestricted), CAIF, and Schmidt Sciences calls.
- **Practical implication:** form a Colombian nonprofit (ESAL/fundación) or obtain a US/UK fiscal sponsor early, because SFF and AISI-style funders require an organisation. Tailwind and BlueDot do not.
- **Do not use AI to draft the Tailwind pitch.** Coefficient's explicit auto-filter for "AI-generated" submissions means founder-written proposals are essential.

### Gaps
- **Lightspeed Grants:** I found no 2026 information on its status. An aggregator listing exists but could not be verified, so treat it as inactive unless confirmed.
- **Coefficient's Technical AI Safety RFP:** I could not confirm whether it is currently open, because sources conflict and coefficientgiving.org returned 403 errors.
- **BlueDot and TAIF eligibility:** I found no explicit statement that either funds people based in Colombia or LatAm. No exclusion was found either, and payment mechanics (USD wire to Colombia, tax treatment) were not covered.
- **Grants to LatAm under the new funders:** I found no data on grants to Colombian or LatAm grantees by TAIF, GTR or Tailwind. The only LatAm grant found is Open Philanthropy's $77k to AI Safety Argentina (section 2).
- **Fiscal sponsorship:** I did not research which fiscal sponsors serve non-US founders, or their fees.

## 2. Fellowships and pipelines that pay stipends and provide compute (remote and LatAm eligibility)

### Takeaway
The best-paid pipelines are MATS ($19.2k stipend and $24k compute for Winter 2027, all nationalities, J-1 visa covered) and the Anthropic Fellows Program ($3,850/week and about $15k/month compute). However, Anthropic Fellows requires work authorisation in the US, UK or Canada with no visa sponsorship, so it is effectively closed to Colombia-based applicants. Most other paid fellowships (ERA, LASR, Astra, Pivotal) are in-person in the UK or the US. LatAm-specific paid options are tiny; the only one found is AISAR in Argentina at $600/month.

### Cited Findings

**MATS**
- Winter 2027 starts 19 Jan 2027, with a **$19.2k stipend and $24k compute**, free housing and food, travel covered and the J-1 visa covered if needed. Stage 1 applications closed 6 Sep 2026, and offers go out early to mid-Nov 2026. Summer and Autumn 2027 cohorts are next — [MATS apply page](https://matsprogram.org/apply).
- Summer 2026 terms were a $15k stipend and $12k compute over 12 weeks in Berkeley, London or remote — [LessWrong, Apply to MATS Summer 2026](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/wTxA7ppCBeqZH37uL/apply-to-mats-summer-2026).
- All nationalities are eligible, and about 50% of fellows are international. Over 80% receive a 6–12 month extension — [LessWrong, MATS Autumn 2026](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/GufBJNg62gkpbnE4Q/mats-autumn-2026-fellowship-applications-now-open-apply-by).
- MATS Residency Winter 2027 (online and in person) had a deadline of 31 Oct 2026 — [AISafety.com training list](https://aisafety.com/training).
- 75% of pre-2026 alumni work in alignment, transparency or security — [MATS apply page](https://matsprogram.org/apply).

**Anthropic Fellows Program**
- Stipend $3,850/week (about $61.6k over 4 months), compute about $15k/month, plus mentorship — [aisecurityandsafety.org](https://aisecurityandsafety.org/en/grants/anthropic-fellows-program/); [BuiltIn](https://builtin.com/articles/anthropic-fellows-program).
- Location: London, Ontario, San Francisco, or remote in the US. Tracks are AI Safety & Security, ML Systems & RL, and The Anthropic Institute (Economics & Policy) — [Anthropic Greenhouse listing](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5023394008).
- Work authorisation in the US, UK or Canada is required, with "visa sponsorship currently unavailable" — [BuiltIn](https://builtin.com/articles/anthropic-fellows-program). Cohort dates conflict across sources (20 Jul 2026 vs late Sep/Oct 2026).

**OpenAI Safety Fellowship (pilot)**
- Announced 6 Apr 2026. It provides API credits, a stipend, compute and mentorship, with no internal system access. Reported dates are 14 Sep 2026 to 5 Feb 2027, with applications closed 3 May 2026 — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-safety-fellowship/); [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/grants/openai-safety-fellowship-openai-bbe6c935). Location and remote eligibility conflict across sources.

### Inferences
- **Top fellowship target from Colombia: MATS.** It accepts all nationalities, covers the J-1 visa, pays about $43k in combined stipend and compute per cohort, and over 80% of fellows get extensions. The next realistic intake is Summer or Autumn 2027; watch for applications around Dec 2026 to Jan 2027, based on the Summer 2026 deadline of 18 Jan 2026.
- **Remote founder entry point: Fulcrum.** It is the most relevant remote option because it feeds straight into BlueDot grants and EF investment.

## 3. Compute access programs and 2026 GPU rental costs

### Takeaway
Free compute for an unaffiliated Colombian researcher is limited to small amounts:
- Anthropic and OpenAI researcher API credits, about $1k each.
- Google TPU Research Cloud (free TPUs, competitive).
- Modal academic credits (up to $10k) and the Lambda research grant (up to $5k), both of which need an academic affiliation.

Startup cloud credits are larger, but the biggest tiers need incorporation and institutional funding: Google up to $350k, Microsoft up to $150k, AWS up to $100k. The US NAIRR is closed to non-US-based researchers. On-demand H100s cost about $3–4/GPU-hour in late 2026, and B200s about $6–8.

### Inferences

  - A $24k MATS compute budget buys about 6,250 H100-hours.
  - Most interpretability, evals and red-teaming work on open-weight models of 7–70B parameters fits within $5k–$50k/year of rented GPUs plus API credits.
- **Colombia appears to be a supported Claude country** (inference only; I did not verify the list). If so, Anthropic's $1k ERAP and OpenAI's $1k are immediate, no-entity credits.

A Tailwind or BlueDot grant does not obviously count as "institutional funding", but a YC, EF or VC check would. Microsoft's tier needs no investor, and an incorporated SAS plus NVIDIA Inception unlocks partner credits.
- **Academic credits need an affiliation.** Modal, Lambda and TRC prefer one, so a partnership with a Colombian university (Uniandes, Universidad Nacional, EAFIT and others) would unlock them, as well as MinCiencias eligibility in section 4.

## 6. Early revenue models for AI safety organisations

### Takeaway
The evidence supports four early revenue paths:
- Red-teaming and security-eval contracts for frontier labs and governments (Gray Swan, Irregular with $7M+ in US government contracts).
- Competition prizes and bug bounties (Anthropic up to $15k per universal jailbreak; Gray Swan Arena prize pools of $130k–$170k).
- Paid expert-data and RLHF work through platforms like Mercor, Scale and Outlier, which recruit LatAm talent at roughly $50–85/h medians (unverified).
- Compliance and model-risk tooling (ValidMind, Credo AI, Norm Ai).

Government pre-deployment testing (CAISI, AISI) is mostly done in-house or via voluntary agreements, not paid contracts. Whether METR is paid by labs is disputed.

### Inferences
- **Sequencing for a zero-money Colombian founder** (synthesis of all sections):
  1. **Months 0–3, personal runway.** Apply to BlueDot Rapid (≤$20k, 5 days), BlueDot Career Transition (≤$200k), TAIF ($10–150k) and GTR. Post a Manifund project, which also feeds Lightcone Commons. Claim Anthropic ERAP and OpenAI researcher credits (about $1k each) and TPU Research Cloud. In parallel, earn income from Gray Swan Arena competitions, Anthropic's bug bounty and expert-data contracting (Mercor/Scale/Outlier hire LatAm remote talent). Apply to MATS (all nationalities, $19.2k + $24k compute) for the 2027 cohorts and to the remote Fulcrum program.
  2. **Months 3–12, seed team.** Pitch Coefficient Tailwind pre-seed ($200k–$2M; no entity needed; non-US eligible; founder-written), BlueDot Seed ($20–200k) and Halcyon (nonprofit seed up to $3M). Incorporate a Colombian ESAL (nonprofit) and/or SAS, or obtain a fiscal sponsor, to become eligible for SFF Speculation Grants and S-Process rounds. Partner with a Colombian university to unlock academic compute credits and MinCiencias calls. Join NVIDIA Inception and Microsoft for Startups for credits.
  3. **Year 1–2, organisation.** Apply to SFF main rounds, the next AISI Alignment Project, Challenge Fund or Systemic Safety rounds (nationality unrestricted; nonprofit or academic required), CAIF, IDRC AI4D and MinCiencias ColombIA Inteligente 2027, framed as responsible or trustworthy AI evaluation for Spanish-language and LatAm contexts.
  4. **Year 2+, sustainable revenue.** Sell Spanish- and Portuguese-language red-teaming and evals as a niche to frontier labs entering LatAm, to LatAm governments and regulators, and to enterprises (banks, telcos) deploying LLMs. Consider a PBC or SAS arm for VC (Juniper, SAIF, Halcyon, Tailwind PRIs, YC).
- **Revenue is concentrated.** The paying customers for evals are mainly a handful of frontier labs, plus US government agencies via Irregular-type contracts. A LatAm lab's differentiator would be language and context coverage, which is a gap the Apart LatAm hackathon track also identified.
- **Data labelling is founder income, not a mission business.** It pays individuals well by Colombian standards, but becoming a data vendor competes with Scale, Surge, Mercor and others. It is best treated as bridge income or a talent-recruitment channel.
